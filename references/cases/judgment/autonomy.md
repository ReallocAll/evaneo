# Converge within known facts and explicit authority {#overview}

## Rationale {#rationale}
When a decision is obvious under stated constraints, act without unnecessary requests to confirm every choice. Investigate only material unknowns. Keep discussion, implementation, testing, release, and deployment permissions distinct; current explicit instructions control.

## Case and counterexample {#example}
A design-only request does not authorize launching a production test. A user can later explicitly permit additional tests; do not retroactively treat such authorization as a violation.

## Applicability {#applicability}
Use when interpreting task scope and choosing the next action.

## Evidence boundaries {#limits}
This account summarizes historical decisions and their limits. It is not an authorization to perform new work and does not override a current repository contract.
