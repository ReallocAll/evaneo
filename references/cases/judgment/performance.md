# Limit performance claims to observed evidence {#overview}

## Rationale {#rationale}
Compare the effective runtime path, matching workloads, corresponding revisions and actual deployment constraints. Sampling percentages, microbenchmarks, readiness indicators, and single-run gains have distinct evidentiary scopes. Preserve negative measurements and test limitations.

## Case and counterexample {#example}
A microbenchmark on a profiler hot spot did not establish game-server TPS gains. A carefully chosen representative workload can support a bounded claim without requiring an expensive repeated matrix.

## Applicability {#applicability}
Use for benchmarking, profiling, performance recommendations, and regressions.

## Evidence boundaries {#limits}
This account summarizes historical decisions and their limits. It is not an authorization to perform new work and does not override a current repository contract.
