## Decision and context {#decision-and-context}

Restore protoc terminology and make a real per-message field-generator cache.

## Observed implementation, tests, and changes {#observed-implementation-tests-and-changes}

class_.py becomes message.py, ClassGenerator becomes MessageGenerator, names.py becomes helpers.py, and imports follow. FieldGeneratorMap constructs one generator for every snapshot field and reuses it for serialize/deserialize rather than reconstructing each twice. Nested combinators and standalone variant aliases still use the factory.

## Rationale and alternatives {#rationale-and-alternatives}

Explicit: mirror upstream architecture and call shapes; the factory alone was not a FieldGeneratorMap. Output identity is reported as the refactor acceptance check.

## Constraints, revisions, and uncertainty {#constraints-revisions-and-uncertainty}

This reverses [#4](0004-89a9452ce7e2.md)'s terminology without reversing the wire model. The new constructor asserts all field types resolved earlier; malformed/unresolved paths may fail sooner, so broad behavior identity is stronger than the observed existing-schema claim.
