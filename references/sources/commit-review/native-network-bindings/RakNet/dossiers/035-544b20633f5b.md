## Engineering finding {#engineering-finding}

BurstTest sends 64-byte x128, 512-byte x64 and 4096-byte x16 batches, checking encoded size/count/index and packet length at least the requested size. ReliableOrderedTest waits explicitly for connection, sends 50 packets on each of 32 channels at 30ms intervals with random 1..5000-byte padding, then checks per-channel sequence and total1600 delivery. This changes the old manual long-running workload and removes allocator logging; it does not benchmark performance. CrossConnection adds three ping-timed connect/disconnect cycles ported from the legacy ConvertTest. It calculates an RTT-based scheduled connection time, requires a connection notification, cancels residual attempts and closes both ends. Randomness is seeded from random_device rather than recording a deterministic seed, so repeatability of a failure would need additional support.
