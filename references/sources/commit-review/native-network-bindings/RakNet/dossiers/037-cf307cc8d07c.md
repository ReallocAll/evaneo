## Engineering finding {#engineering-finding}

BigPacketTest replaces an83,296,256-byte interactive transfer with50,000/500,000/5,000,000-byte cases. The first two compare every payload byte; the largest checks length only. It waits for both connection notifications, checks Send returned nonzero, and uses ACK_RECEIPT reliability without asserting the receipt. The progress counter is incremented but not asserted. ServerClientTest creates100 clients: one test requires all100 server-side connections, while the traffic test starts with at least50 and ends with at least one connection; traffic is drained without validating payload or counts. RelayPlugin tests duplicate registration, named message content and group content, with detach-before-destroy cleanup. Its WaitForRelayResponse returns from inside a for-loop whose increment deallocates the packet, skipping that cleanup on the success path; this is a static ownership concern, not a reproduced leak. The shorter representative cases are a scope/cost tradeoff, not evidence of inadequate engineering by themselves.
