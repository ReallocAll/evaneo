## Engineering finding {#engineering-finding}

Replaces RakNetLibStatic/RakNetDLL with one RakNet target controlled by standard BUILD_SHARED_LIBS, defaultOFF. Internal sample/extension links follow it; _RAKNET_DLL versus _RAKNET_LIB definitions remain conditional. Exports and public system dependencies retained. Removes ability to build both variants in one configuration; no reason beyond simplification in message.
